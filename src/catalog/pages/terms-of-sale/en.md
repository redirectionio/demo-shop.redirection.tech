Maison 301 is a fictional shop: no sales contract can be concluded on this website, and no order is shipped.

## Orders

An order placed on this website has no value: no payment is requested and no data is kept. The cart is only stored in your browser.

## Prices

Prices are in euros, all taxes included, for illustration purposes only.
