title: Delivery and returns
description: Free delivery on orders over €100, free returns within 30 days.

## Delivery

Orders are prepared within 48 hours. Delivery is free on orders over €100, and costs €9.90 below that.

- Small items: home or pick-up point delivery, within 3 to 5 working days.
- Furniture: delivery by appointment, within 2 to 3 weeks.

## Returns

You have 30 days after delivery to return a product, in its original packaging. Returns are free and refunds are issued within 14 days.
