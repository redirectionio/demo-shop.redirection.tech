Maison 301 was born from a simple idea: a home is built piece by piece, with objects you keep for a long time.

## Our selection

We choose every product for its craftsmanship, its materials and its durability: solid wood, washed linen, wool, stoneware, blown glass. We work with workshops we know, and we prefer a tight collection to an endless catalog.

## A fictional shop

Maison 301 doesn't exist: this website is a [redirection.io](https://redirection.io) demo, used to show how to manage the redirections, 404 errors and traffic of an e-commerce website. No product is sold and no order is shipped.
